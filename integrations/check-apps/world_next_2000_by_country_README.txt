WORLD NEXT 2,000 WEBSITES — COUNTRY-FOCUSED EXTENSION

Files:
- world_next_2000_by_country.txt: 2,000 domain names, one per line, no headers.
- world_next_2000_by_country.csv: the same 2,000 domains with a country/market label.

Selection notes:
- Domains were curated from widely used national news, commerce, banking, government, travel, education, telecom, jobs, and local-service sites across multiple markets.
- Exact domains already present in world_top_2000_websites.txt were excluded. Obvious subdomains were collapsed to the registrable/root domain where possible.
- Obvious same-brand country variants were conservatively filtered when the brand label matched an existing first-list domain; selected cross-country families were also deduplicated within this new list.
- Country names indicate the primary market association used for grouping, not the server's physical location.
- This is a practical country-focused testing extension, NOT a certified next-2,000 global traffic ranking. Country-level rankings use different methods and cannot be merged into a precise worldwide rank without a common licensed traffic dataset.
- The public NetAPI country rankings state that they cover 217 countries, with traffic data updated monthly and the ordering rebuilt daily: https://netapi.com/top-websites/
