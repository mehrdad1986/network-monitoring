# 11. DESIGN GUIDANCE FOR THE VPN
################################################################################
TRANSPORT
  - TCP 443 or TCP 80 only. UDP/443 to Cloudflare is filtered; every IPsec/
    OpenVPN/WireGuard/L2TP port is filtered to arbitrary destinations.
  - Cloudflare-proxied front is the right architecture: it survives IP-level
    blocking and gives you many anycast entry points.

NAMING — THE DECISION FOR YOUR IRANCELL DEPLOYMENT
  - Your EXISTING workers.dev VLESS front PASSES at 80% here. It is not dead on
    Irancell the way it was on MCI. That is the single biggest practical change.
  - But 20% failure is not a usable tunnel. Options, in order of preference:
      a) KEEP workers.dev but design for failure: aggressive retry, multiple
         parallel connections, connection racing. Cheap, no infrastructure.
      b) 5-or-more-label hostname on your own Cloudflare zone. On MCI this was
         MANDATORY (workers.dev hard-blocked). On Irancell it is still the more
         robust choice because it drops OUT of the matcher's window entirely
         rather than relying on a probabilistic leak.
      c) If you take (b), verify before distributing: test your real host AND a
         non-existent subdomain of the same zone. If the fake name also fails,
         the whole zone is filtered — rotate.
  - Do NOT ship raw Cloudflare edge IPs. You pin yourself to one filtered edge.

DNS
  - Address resolvers by IP LITERAL. https://8.8.8.8/dns-query and
    https://8.8.4.4/dns-query are confirmed working; 9.9.9.9 also serves.
  - CARRY ALL THREE. The working set is INVERTED between MCI and Rightel, so a
    single hardcoded resolver is a portability bug.
  - NEVER pin a resolver IP in dns.hosts while keeping the resolver hostname as
    the SNI — measured RESET. This breaks bootstrap and kills the tunnel.
  - DoT/853 is unavailable; do not build on it.

RELIABILITY (this is where Irancell differs from MCI)
  - Assume ~20% connection failure on any 4-label workers.dev name and build
    retry/racing into the client. A single successful test is not evidence of a
    working configuration — every verdict in this dossier is a pass RATE.
  - Validate any config with n>=15 trials, not one connection. One-shot testing
    produced a wrong answer on the very first real worker tested.

HYGIENE
  - Unset all proxy env vars before measuring. A dead SOCKS proxy was configured
    system-wide in an earlier session and silently corrupted every result.
  - Before shipping, re-run the battery on each ISP you care about. The filter
    differs per operator in ways that will break assumptions.

# 12. STILL UNKNOWN — DO NOT FILL THESE IN
################################################################################
  - Whether the probabilistic behaviour on Irancell is a sampling filter, a
    per-flow state machine, or Cloudflare edge variance. The controls at 100%
    (cloudflare.com, workers.dev apex) argue the drops are filter-side, not
    edge-side, but the mechanism is not established.
  - Why 3-label passes 93% and 4-label passes 80%. Consistent with a sampling
    rate that scales with match specificity, but the rule is not characterised.
  - Whether the matcher is a regex, a label counter, or a fixed byte window.
    Only the observable boundary is mapped.
  - Whether hop 8 and the SNI filter are the SAME device or two devices.
  - Whether QUIC/HTTP3 is filtered to non-Cloudflare destinations. Only
    Cloudflare was solidly shown blocked.
  - Whether the workers.dev rule is MCI/Irancell-specific or a national list that
    Rightel simply does not apply. A fourth network (Shatel, Irancell fixed line,
    a reseller) would settle it.
  - The exact TLS record-layer mechanism behind the DoH-by-name failures. They
    presented as timeouts here; Rightel showed record-layer failures. Possibly
    two mechanisms.
  - Whether the case-sensitivity effect is genuine or partly a Cloudflare
    behaviour. The SERVED cases prove Cloudflare tolerates mixed case, so an
    effect exists, but not cleanly separable in magnitude.
  - Vendor, model and line rate. Unanswerable from the client side.
