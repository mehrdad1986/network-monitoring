Cloudflare IP 172.67.163.235 (HTTP 200) but is BLOCKED on 104.21.66.195

  1. Client connects to 104.21.66.195 (blocked) instead of 172.67.163.235 (works)
  2. ECH bootstrap fetches config from dns.google (poisoned) -> fails

================================================================================
IMPLICATIONS FOR THESIS
================================================================================

NEW FINDING: The DPI uses at least TWO distinct filtering mechanisms:

  Mechanism A: SNI wildcard rules (e.g., *.workers.dev with label-count window)
    - Applies to ALL destination IPs
    - Stateless, pattern-based
    - Measured in previous sessions (2026-10-02)

  Mechanism B: Targeted (IP, SNI) combination rules (e.g., islam.janex.ir on 104.21.66.195)
    - Applies to SPECIFIC IP+SNI pairs
    - Suggests the DPI can target individual domains by their primary CDN IP
    - NEW: not documented in previous sessions

This implies the DPI maintains a per-(IP, SNI) blocklist for reported/targeted
domains, in addition to namespace-wide wildcard rules. The secondary CDN IP
(172.67.163.235) is not yet flagged - a cat-and-mouse dynamic where blocking
the primary IP drives users to the secondary, which may be flagged later.

Note: The previous report's conclusion "Is it IP-based? NO" was correct for
workers.dev (pure SNI wildcard) but INCOMPLETE for custom domains, which can
be targeted via (IP, SNI) combination rules.
